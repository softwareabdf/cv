About Me

I'm Abdelrahman Fathi Hassan, from Sudan, and I live in Makkah. I studied Software Engineering at SEGi University in Malaysia, in partnership with the University of Central Lancashire in the UK — so I came into construction with a programmer's way of thinking.

Since 2021 I've worked for Al Rajhi Building & Construction in three cities: Riyadh, Dammam and Makkah. Along the way I've taken on document control, accounts, procurement, the warehouse and IT. Each role added to the one before, and I've kept all of them.

What I enjoy most is bringing order to busy projects — and when something is repetitive, writing a small tool so nobody has to do it by hand again. I like being the person the team can rely on, whatever the task.
